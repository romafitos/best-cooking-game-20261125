# Wok & Ember — concept tournament (16 candidates, scored vs the judge's 5 criteria)

Judge criteria (in order): (1) complete cooking game visible in screenshots —
kitchen + order board + ingredients on counter + dish mid-assembly + timer +
plate ready to serve; (2) fun/depth — route planning, combining recipes,
growing pressure; (3) feel/polish — instant chop/plate feedback, felt timer,
readable rush; (4) look/originality — food worth serving, readable counters,
own look, no commercial copy; (5) structure — files with one job each.

Scored 0–5 per criterion (C1..C5), max 25. Anti-cliche ban: no Overcooked
clone names, no "bug is a feature", no deleting-failing-test jokes.

Head-to-head (champion vs runners-up, blinded on criteria):
- #1 vs #2 Ramen Rally: ramen bowls all look alike in a still; wok line shows
  flame + steam + chop + plate in ONE frame. #1 wins 4–1.
- #1 vs #3 Sushi Sprint: sushi has no cooking heat — nothing "mid-assembly"
  reads as cooking; wok toss + flame reads instantly. #1 wins 4–1.
- #1 vs #15 Midnight Bistro: bistro is elegant but slow/precious; night market
  gives urgency + warm palette + route planning across 4 stations. #1 wins 3–2.
- #1 vs #8 Taco Tornado: tacos are assemble-only (no cook step); wok adds a
  timed cook with burn risk = real depth. #1 wins 4–1.

CHAMPION: #1 Wok & Ember — Night Market Kitchen.
Differentiator vs the likely field (starter-derived + generic diner clones):
the cover frame shows ALL FIVE criterion-1 elements at once (ticket rail,
ingredient crates, chopping board with chopped goods, wok mid-toss with flame,
timer, plated dish on the pass) — most entries show two or three. Depth axis:
6 recipes × 8 ingredients × 3 processing chains (chop / wok / steam) with
route planning across a 4-station kitchen and two rush waves; combo streaks
reward planning, not chores.
